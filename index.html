About this journal

Written by someone who reads slowly on purpose

Grounds & Margins started as a set of notes I kept beside my coffee pot — half brewing log, half reading journal. It's grown into a small weekly habit: one entry about something I made, read, or noticed closely enough to write down.

Most weeks it's coffee and books. Some weeks it's just the weather and what I was thinking about. There's no schedule to keep and no algorithm to please — just the quiet hour before the day gets loud.
